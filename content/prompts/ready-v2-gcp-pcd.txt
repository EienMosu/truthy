- No truism: a statement that is true by common sense, by definition or by arithmetic, and that is not specific to this subject, teaches nothing. Write a different card.

Requirement and coverage words ("requires", "must", "each", "every", "automatically", "by default", "directly") must not mark false statements: use them about as often in true statements as in false ones.

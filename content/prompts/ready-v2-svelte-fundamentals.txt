- State traps: $state deeply proxies arrays and simple objects, not class instances. Class fields can explicitly use $state. - Derived and effect traps: $derived tracks reactive reads during evaluation, recalculates when next read after dependencies change, and does not itself deep-proxy its result. Test $derived for computed state, not effect loops that synchronize two pieces of state. Sources: https://svelte.dev/docs/svelte/$derived and https://svelte.dev/docs/svelte/$effect.

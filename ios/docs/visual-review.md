  - Not settled, for the owner: on the SE the score label "Correct in a row" breaks one word earlier on iOS ("Correct in / a row" against "Correct in a / row"), and the comparison beside it too ("First round on / this route" against "First round on this / route"). This pass took it for a fraction of a point in the column's width and the step 52 review for the field label drawn at 11; with every role at its exact size (below) the label's column is 109.6 wide and holds "Correct in a" (82.7), as the web's does, and the breaks stay. The cause is SwiftUI's line breaking, which moves a word down rather than leave one word alone on a last line (Apple's standard line-break strategy, push-out); CSS breaks greedily. Measured with TextKit at the same widths: no strategy gives the web's lines, push-out gives iOS's. SwiftUI has no setting for it (it ignores a paragraph style's `lineBreakStrategy`); drawing such text through UIKit with no strategy would match the web. It shows only where the web's last line holds one word.
  - Largest text (N9): nothing clipped outside a scroll view, no word broken inside the word; the ticket and the result scroll under the action row as on the web. Nothing looks like a web page in a frame (spec 5.7). This pass missed what the step 52 review found at the largest size (below).
